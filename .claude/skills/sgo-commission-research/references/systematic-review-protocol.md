# Systematic-review protocol

1. Commit a preregistration before searching.
2. Lock inclusion and exclusion criteria; record later deviations.
3. Search at least two independent indices for Tier II and III work.
4. Preserve complete query, date, result-count, screening, and exclusion logs.
5. Apply the study-appropriate risk-of-bias framework.
6. Synthesize only the verified included corpus.
7. Report a PRISMA-style identification-to-inclusion flow.

Tier III work requires a second-rater sample. Every AI-assisted screening,
extraction, synthesis, and verification step must be disclosed.
