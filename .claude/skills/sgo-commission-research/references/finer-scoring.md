# FINER scoring

Score candidate research questions from 1 to 5 on:

| Dimension | Test |
|---|---|
| Feasible | Can the evidence be obtained and verified within the request? |
| Interesting | Will the answer move an owner organ's practice or theory? |
| Novel | Does it add more than existing SGO work? |
| Ethical | Does collection respect privacy, consent, and SGO doctrine? |
| Relevant | Does it answer the commissioning owner's actual need? |

| Tier | Minimum total | Per-dimension floor |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paper | 15 | 2 |
| Thesis | 19 | 3 |
| Dissertation | 22 | 4 |

The Question Architect proposes scores; the Provost ratifies them. A request
below either threshold must be reshaped before provider routing.
