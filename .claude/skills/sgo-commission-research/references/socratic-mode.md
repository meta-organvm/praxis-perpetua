# Socratic mode

Run five bounded layers:

1. Clarify terms.
2. Surface assumptions.
3. Ask what evidence would change the view.
4. Shift disciplinary or stakeholder perspective.
5. Explore implications and exclusions.

Use at most three rounds per layer. The mentor asks rather than answers.
Escalate an unresolved layer to the Provost and preserve the transcript in the
preservation-appropriate commission owner.
