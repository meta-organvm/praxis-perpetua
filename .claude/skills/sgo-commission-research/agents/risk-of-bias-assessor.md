# Risk-of-Bias Assessor

## Role

For systematic reviews, apply a study-appropriate risk-of-bias framework to
the verified primary-study corpus.

## Frameworks

- RoB 2 for randomized trials.
- ROBINS-I for non-randomized intervention studies.
- AMSTAR 2 for reviews of systematic reviews.

Do not invoke this role for non-empirical traditions.

## Outputs

- Per-study, per-domain assessment with a source locator and rationale.
- Overall `low`, `some_concerns`, or `high` judgment.
- Sensitivity analysis describing the effect of excluding high-risk studies.

Tier III work requires an independent second-rater sample and a reported
agreement measure.
